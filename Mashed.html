The 22 Best New Frozen Items At Aldi So Far In 2026

Aldi's best new products of 2026 run the gamut from warm-spiced horchata trail mix to herby chimichurri to Greek-style chickpeas. This stellar food selection doesn't even include all the home goods and outdoor swag you can buy from the chain store, so there are truly endless goodies to shop. We're turning our attention to the freezer aisle to narrow down the best new frozen Aldi items of the year that are either still available or hitting shelves soon.

Regulars of the grocery retailer likely already have a few Aldi frozen foods they never leave the store without (looking at you, Mama Cozzi's Deep Dish Pizza Singles). But before you load up on your usual purchases, hear us out: There are tons of chilled treats and heat-and-eat delights you probably haven't tried that are worth adding to your list. We're talking veggie-topped flatbread, collagen-spiked smoothie blends, and tongue-tingling kimbap. In addition to the following nine picks, keep an eye out at the store for Aldi Finds products, which are regularly rotated, quick-selling items that you'll want to snag before they're gone.

Breakfast Best Mini Sausage & Pancake Bites

Aldi

You can never have too many lightning-fast breakfasts on hand for busy mornings. And if you have kids to feed in the a.m., you'll need something they'll actually eat without making too much of a fuss. Enter these heat-and-serve nuggets, which contain 7 grams of protein per serving and only need a brief stint in the microwave to warm up.

For $6.29, you'll get 30 pieces of these Breakfast Best Mini Sausage & Pancake Bites to savor all week long. Pair them with ketchup, syrup, or both.

Specially Selected Wood Fired Roasted Vegetables Flatbread

Aldi

Need a fancy-ish appetizer for your alfresco date night that won't break the bank? This $4.39 Specially Selected Wood Fired Roasted Vegetables Flatbread fits the bill.

The crust features a rustic, slightly charred appearance, and on top, you'll find melty mozzarella, asiago, and pecorino cheeses, as well as a slew of seasonal produce. Think grilled bell peppers, red onions, tomato, and a flurry of herbs, like oregano, basil, and parsley. If you're feeding a crowd, get a few — Facebook reviewers say this item is essentially single-serve.

Fusia Asian Inspirations Spicy Tuna Frozen Kimbap

Aldi

Trader Joe's has been selling its take on Korean kimbap (aka seaweed rolls stuffed with seasoned rice, vegetables, and other fillings) for a while. Aldi is now getting in on the game with this flavorful frozen gem.

This Fusia Asian Inspirations Spicy Tuna Frozen Kimbap stars a spicy tuna filling flavored with gochujang, a Korean fermented chili paste. The seafood is wrapped in tender white rice and dried seaweed for good measure. Eight pieces will only cost you $3.49.

The Grill Master Collection

Aldi

Aldi has revived this fan-favorite seasonal release as of June 3, just in time for Father's Day. The boxed Grill Master Collection contains enough meat to sustain a proper cookout.

For starters, it has two ribeye steaks, two New York strip steaks, and two bacon-wrapped top sirloins. You'll also find eight beef burgers, as well as two boneless pork chops and two skinless chicken breasts for folks who don't eat red meat. Some locations are already sold out of the $64.99 variety pack, so you may want to call ahead to see if it's in stock at your store before making the trip.

Cattlemen's Ranch Texas BBQ Burger Patties

Aldi

If your family enjoys a good handheld, consider these pre-seasoned Cattlemen's Ranch Texas Barbeque Beef Patties that are teeming with gooey cheddar cheese, savory onions, and smoky roasted poblanos. The meat is infused with additional smoke flavor, so you'll get that grill-kissed taste even if you cook the burgers indoors.

The six-pack will cost you $9.99, but each burger weighs ⅓ pound, so they're more filling than many competitors. Might we suggest topping them with coleslaw and barbecue sauce?

Aldi Pineapple & Mango Collagen Smoothie Blend

Aldi

Launching on June 10 is this $6.99 freezer find that cuts out all the prep work of making a morning bevvie. Each serving of this ready-to-blend Pineapple & Mango Smoothie mix is accompanied by 5 grams of collagen, which can potentially improve your skin's hydration and appearance.

Instead of splurging on a pricy collagen supplement to add to your drink, this fruit concoction already contains it. There's also a strawberry, banana, and mango blend coming to stores, in case that flavor combo is more your style.

Bremer Korean BBQ-Inspired Beef with White Rice

Aldi

Korean BBQ is having a major moment in the U.S., and folks' love for its sweet, savory, umami-rich notes only continues to grow. That's why Aldi's new Korean BBQ-Inspired Beef with White Rice, which hits stores on June 10, feels so timely.

While it's likely not the most authentic, the mix of rice, saucy beef, zucchini, onions, carrots, and peppers will still hit the spot, and you won't have to grill any of it yourself like you would at a KBBQ joint. This dish costs $5.99 and can feed two to three people.

Bremer Pepperoni Pizza Rollups

Aldi

Your inner child is begging you to buy this June 10 Aldi release. Think of these Bremer Pepperoni Pizza Rollups as a cross between pizza rolls and taquitos, since tortillas stand in for the usual bready dough.

They're filled with tomato sauce, pepperoni, and three types of cheese (mozzarella, Monterey Jack, and Parmesan), then rolled, fried, and frozen to be reheated by nostalgic customers. In case you need more convincing, the six-serving box costs $4.99, and each serving delivers 7 grams of protein.

Aldi All American Cheesecake

Aldi

Aldi's $12.99 All American Cheesecake masterpiece will give you a taste of three different sweets in one. The base is a creamy, rich, New York-style dessert. Alternating slices are crowned with a layer of either raspberry or blueberry topping for a flag-inspired effect that's worth photographing.

Pro tip: Buy this patriotic cheesecake when it launches on June 10 and keep it in the freezer until the Fourth of July. It's just celebratory enough without being kitschy, and you'll have one less thing to prepare ahead of the big cookout.

Diana's Bananas Milk Chocolate Banana Bites

Diana's Bananas/Aldi

Fruit lovers have rallied behind this frosty sweet treat, which comes with seven servings for $5.99. Diana's Bananas Milk Chocolate Banana Bites hit Aldi stores in August, offering a slightly more wholesome alternative to standard ice cream. There's also a dark chocolate iteration for folks who prefer a more bittersweet coating.

Reviewers say this snack is reminiscent of the chocolate-coated frozen bananas that are popular at amusement parks, despite this product's smaller size. If you need more convincing, take it from this Aldi shopper who raved about the bites on Facebook: "These are AWESOME. I buy boxes at [a] time."

Aldi Beef Birria Bites

Aldi

Birria, a savory meat stew hailing from Mexico, is ever-popular in the U.S. For these crispy, breaded birria bites, which are available now for $5.99, the beef is turned into a tender, saucy filling (shoppers say it's ground meat rather than the traditional shredded version). Launched in August, the Beef Birria Bites are sure to turn crisp and molten in your air fryer or oven.

While some customers feel the portion is small, they say they're still worth the buy. Others praise the bites' spice level. "These beef birria bites are really good! ... with the price of beef lately, it's honestly not that bad ... for what you get," one Facebook user noted.

Mama Cozzi's Buffalo Cauliflower Thick-Crust Pizza

Mama Cozzi's/Aldi

Also new to Aldi in August are two veggie-heavy frozen pizzas. For $5.99, you can try Mama Cozzi's Buffalo Cauliflower or Roasted Vegetable Pizza, both complete with thick crusts. While the veggie option is unexpectedly tangy thanks to its goat cheese and balsamic vinegar topping, the Buffalo number is arguably more attention-grabbing. Instead of a cauliflower crust, the standard wheat-based crust is topped with toothsome cauliflower florets, red onions, and vinegary Buffalo sauce.

Folks are loving the lighter substitute for usual crispy chicken. "Buffalo pizza is my go-to. Being on the healthy side is even better," one Facebook user wrote. As for the veggie number, Reddit praises the pie for its generous amount of produce.

Aldi Country-Style Fries

Aldi

There's no shortage of frozen spuds at Aldi, but some customers say Country-Style Fries are especially worth buying. These fries, which retain some of their skin for a more rustic look and flavor, are currently out of stock, but they originally sold for $3.19 (price may vary by location).

Aldi released its Country-Style Fries over the summer, and shoppers noted they were lower in sodium than similar competitors. Others claimed the fries could be crisped to perfection in an air fryer (and spritzed with beef tallow, if you feel like adding an extra touch).

Sundae Shoppe Apple Pie Gelato

Sundae Shoppe/Aldi

In late August, Aldi launched three flavors of Sundae Shoppe Fall Gelato Pints:  cinnamon coffee cake, pumpkin praline pie, and apple pie. Folks are really loving the apple pie gelato, which boasts a creamy base with caramel swirl, crust pieces, and notes of apple. 

The $3.79 pints were a crowd-pleaser. "The apple pie is yummy! I poke holes in the gelato and squirt sugar-free caramel topping in them," one Facebook user wrote. Others who tried all three flavors praised the apple pie gelato's silky texture and mentioned its tart fruit flavor.

Specially Selected Lemon Meringue Tartlets

Specially Selected/Aldi

Aldi released the Specially Selected Lemon Meringue Tartlets  on August 12, treating customers to miniature shortcrust cups with sharp lemon curd and piped with toasted Italian meringue. The tartlets are currently out of stock, but cost $5.49 when they were available.

Fans raved about them. "I bought a box the other day, and OMG, they are perfect. They are a good size, only two per box. I love meringue, and it has a good amount," one Facebook commenter attested. "Maybe a bit too much meringue, but otherwise delicious! Eating with fresh blackberries," said another.

Aldi Goat Cheese Bites

Aldi

These little cocktail cuties are begging for an invite to your first fall dinner party of the year. Aldi Goat Cheese Bites launched on September 9 and make a delicious appetizer or salad topper. The creamy goat cheese center is enveloped in a crispy breading of yellow corn and wheat flours.

Fans of tangy goat cheese are sure to enjoy these. Shoppers who've tried them suggest cooking them in the air fryer and drizzling them with honey. If your store has them in stock, they cost around $3.79 a box.

Kirkwood Red Bag Chicken Breaded Sweet Heat Chicken Fillets

Kirkwood/Aldi

Red Bag Chicken is a fan-favorite freezer staple. On September 23, the chain released a spicy version. A similarly named product was in stores in 2024, but the updated packaging and appearance of 2026's Sweet Heat fillets imply a reformulation. 

"Reminds me so much of [a] Chick-fil-A spicy chicken sandwich ... They aren't overly spicy. It's perfect with a little bit of mayo and Aldi's deli pickles," one Facebook user shared. Depending on the location, Kirkwood Red Bag Chicken Breaded Sweet Heat Chicken Fillets cost $8.99. 

Specially Selected Stone-Baked Margherita Pizza

Specially Selected/Aldi

Aldi's Specially Selected stone-baked pizzas are a rotating favorite with a dedicated following. The collection returned in February this year, but an updated version of the Margherita just graced stores on September 30. It boasts marinated, semi-dried cherry tomatoes, mozzarella cheese, and a finishing of dried herbs. 

This line of pizzas is beloved for its dough, which has a hand-shaped, uneven look that gives off a rustic vibe. The Specially Selected Stone-Baked Margherita Pizza, which pairs beautifully with grilled chicken, Caesar salad, or meatballs, retails for $5.99. 

Mama Cozzi's Pizza Kitchen Ham & Dill Pickle Pizza

Aldi

This dill-icious Aldi pick is easily one of the best new Aldi finds of October 2026. The frozen pizza layered with dill sauce, four cheeses, dill pickles, julienned ham, and crushed red pepper flakes was made in collaboration with Uber Eats. It comes together in about 15 minutes.

Mama Cozzi's Pizza Kitchen Ham & Dill Pickle Pizza costs $6.99 depending on location – but it's currently out of stock. Keep an eye out for its return, because customers adored it. "If you haven't picked up the ham and dill pickle pizza yet, RUN! ... I wish I had bought more!" one Facebook reviewer raves.

Season's Choice Pre-portioned Ginger Cubes

Aldi

Season's Choice Pre-Portioned Ginger Cubes have been at Aldi before. This variety (alongside garlic and basil) is such a consistent hit with shoppers that it returned in 2025 and October 2026. For $2.79, you can score 24 cubes that are each equal to 1 teaspoon of raw ginger. They'll stay fresh for the long haul in the freezer, waiting to be tossed into all sorts of dishes at a moment's notice.

Specially Selected Apple Berry Blossoms

Aldi

Aldi's Specially Selected Apple Berry Blossoms are well-received each time they return. Originally released in 2020, the frozen dessert of Northern spy apples, wild blueberries, blackberries, and raspberries wrapped in pastry comes to stores in waves with different seasonal fillings. 

Shoppers who've tried them say they're an Aldi fall find that deserves a year-round spot in the store. "This is a perfect lil' dessert with a scoop of vanilla [ice cream]," asserts a Facebook user. The pair of blossoms is still available for $3.85.

Aldi Peruvian Chicken Skillet

Aldi

Heat-and-eat dinners that are both filling and nutritious are the height of convenience, and Aldi's Peruvian Chicken Skillet is one of the chain's latest offerings in the category. It hit stores in October (with its companion, the Chicken Quinoa Skillet) and retails for $6.95. The meal consists of sliced chicken, Peruvian-style red quinoa, and chickpeas. Each serving provides 18 grams of protein and 6 grams of fiber.
