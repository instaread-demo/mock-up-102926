The experts advised the UK Government that the ‘crisis’ infection should no longer be classified as a ‘high consequence infectious disease’. In other words covid was officially no worse than the flu.

Just days after the significance of the infection had been officially downgraded, the British Government ignored its own advisors and published an Emergency Bill which gave the police extraordinary new powers and put millions of people under house arrest. I revealed all this in my bookComing Apocalypsein April 2020 No one ever questioned the truth but I was censored, reviled and demonised.

The fake covid pandemic was being used to prepare people for Net Zero and the Great Reset.No one was to be allowed to disrupt that preparation.

More worrying than the libels I endured was the fact that although I was banned from all social media sites, rogues were allowed to set up accounts in my name. My complaints were ignored. Even more worrying was the sending of fake emails in my name.

I received numerous death threats after I exposed the covid fraud and the truth about the vaccine. By good fortune we sent the car to a specialist garage for a routine service and that saved our lives. The garage reported that two Pirelli tyres had been ‘cut to cords’. I have the garage report to prove that the damage was done.

My central argument has remained essentially unchanged: the fundamental problem lies in what is presented to the public as “medical science,” and in treating medical expertise as automatically equivalent to expertise in experimental science.

    It appears that this concern is finally receiving greater attention. In my view, the most important question is therefore broader than COVID itself: Are the underlying claims being established according to appropriate experimental scientific standards, and are those making and evaluating those claims appropriately trained to do so?
