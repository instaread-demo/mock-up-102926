The Case Of The Fifth Word Class 8 Summary

Summary of the Story

The story is about Encyclopedia Brown, a boy detective who lives in Idaville, USA. His father, Chief Brown, is the police chief. Whenever Chief Brown faces a difficult case, he discusses it at the dinner table, and Encyclopedia solves it before dinner ends. In this case, Chief Brown talks about Tim Nolan, a man suspected of a jewellery robbery years ago.

Nolan had a friend, Daniel Davenport, and both were believed to have hidden the stolen jewellery. Nolan died of a stroke and left everything to Davenport in his will. The mystery is how Nolan’s will and past connect to the robbery. Encyclopedia listens carefully and uses his sharp mind to solve the puzzle.

Summary in Hindi

यह कहानी एन्साइक्लोपीडिया ब्राउन नामक एक बाल जासूस की है। उसके पिता चीफ ब्राउन पुलिस प्रमुख हैं। जब भी उन्हें कोई कठिन केस मिलता है, वे रात के खाने पर चर्चा करते हैं और एन्साइक्लोपीडिया उसे हल कर देता है। इस केस में टिम नोलन नामक व्यक्ति की मृत्यु हो जाती है।

वह पहले एक ज्वेलरी चोरी में शामिल था और अपने दोस्त डेनियल डेवनपोर्ट के साथ मिलकर गहने छिपा दिए थे। मरने से पहले नोलन ने अपनी वसीयत में सब कुछ डेवनपोर्ट को दे दिया। एन्साइक्लोपीडिया अपनी बुद्धि से इस रहस्य को सुलझाता है।

Theme of the Story

The theme is intelligence and observation. It shows how careful thinking and knowledge can solve mysteries that even adults find difficult.

About the Author

The story is written by Donald J. Sobol, an American author famous for the Encyclopedia Brown detective series for children.

Characters in the Story

  • Encyclopedia Brown (Leroy) – Young detective.
  • Chief Brown – His father, police chief.
  • Mrs. Brown – His mother.
  • Tim Nolan – Suspect in jewellery robbery, later dies.
  • Daniel Davenport – Nolan’s friend, beneficiary of his will.

Moral of the Story

The moral is that sharp observation and logical thinking are powerful tools. Age does not matter when it comes to intelligence.
